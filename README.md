Completed as part of the **JPMorgan Chase & Co. Quantitative Research Job Simulation** on Forage. This repository contains pricing engines for commodity storage assets, credit risk modeling, and optimal binning algorithms for borrower credit evaluation.

---

## 📌 Modules
1. **Natural Gas Commodity Storage Contract Valuation (`commodity_storage_pricing.py`)**:
   - Models physical gas storage facilities under price spreads and forward curves.
   - Evaluates cash flows under injection, withdrawal, and storage constraints.
2. **Optimal FICO Score Bucketing (`fico_score_bucketing.py`)**:
   - Binning algorithm grouping borrower FICO scores into discrete risk bands.
   - Maximizes Information Value (IV) and discriminatory power for default prediction.

